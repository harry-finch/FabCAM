# ADR-0011: Bounded normalized cutting-reference adaptation

Status: Implemented under authorized FS3.9; human checkpoint approval pending
Date: 2026-10-13

## Context

Exact applicability rejects metric cutters near two licensed imperial anchors.
Softwood Pocket also differs in material identity, machine and engagement. Removing
all gates would silently generalize source data and physically scoped lab evidence.

## Decision

KEEP legacy Engine mathematics and exact rows. EXTEND pack resolution with a
separate derived layer, versioned policy and schema-3 opt-in transfer declarations.
Only reference-category ReferenceData may adapt. Diameter/DOC normalization stays
within strict categorical scope; explicit reviewed mappings permit softwood/MDF,
confirmed upcut1F series, fablab-router class and conservative full-slot basis for
nominal Pocket. No engagement speed increase, new physical limits or evidence
promotion. Bounded nearest/extrapolation and within-series normalized interpolation
share existing clamps/rounding. Preserve originals and both interpolation anchors.

Quality, evidence and mode remain separate. Taxonomy fallback stays explicit;
missing families/theoretical profiles remain missing. Stable trace and generated
coverage expose rejected dimensions. Details and numeric formulas are in
[matching policy](../feeds-speeds/FS3_9_MATCHING.md).

## Alternatives considered

Nearest raw row copying, broad wood/unknown-machine equivalence, 1F→2F conversion,
silent Pocket equivalence, proprietary dataset scraping and changing existing exact
feeds are rejected. Pure theoretical values without authored data are also rejected.

## Consequences

No Tool/CuttingParameters, post or geometry redesign. Additive milling-specific
metadata requires schema/TS/WASM rebuild. Old packs/saves still load; older loaders
reject schema3. New public pack version keeps original six rows and CC BY-SA terms;
prior source version remains for regression. No private pack auto-population.
Applied/manual values remain authoritative. FS4 remains DEFERRED and NONBLOCKING.

## Validation

Exact six-row parity; relative-near/far and both diameter directions; normalized
interpolation with two originals; deterministic order; categorical refusals;
machine penalty; existing parent precedence, disagreement, privacy and evidence;
explicit Apply and reopen; generated coverage and browser inspection. Physical
observations may later refine bounded policy under FS4, without redesigning storage.
