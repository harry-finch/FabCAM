## Obtuse T-bone geometry

Put the vertex at zero, with unit edge rays u and w, radius r and disk center r*u.
A point t*w lies on the disk exactly when:

`|t*w-r*u|²-r² = t² - 2*r*t*cos(angle) = 0`.

At an obtuse angle, the second root `2*r*cos(angle)` is negative. Thus there is
no second intersection on the **forward** edge ray (t>0); the vertex is the only
contact. A non-tangent junction at the vertex is not itself a failure of this
Boolean relief geometry. Both T-bone directions retain the exact cutter radius;
the existing offset-reachability test is extended to 90.1, 120 and 135 degrees,
and an independent algebraic disk/ray test covers the alleged intersection.
This is ideal geometry, not a claim about runout or machining tolerance. Acute
corners remain rejected by the existing policy.
